InfyExchange Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

InfyExchange is a marketplace where Infosys Mysore trainees can sell, rent, lend or give away items and post requests for things they need. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your phone number, role and joining date, which you give us when you register or complete your profile.
  • A password, if you sign up with email and password. We store only a hashed version, never the password itself.
  • The items and requests you post, including their descriptions and up to five photos each.
  • Enquiries you send or receive about items.

Sign in with Google

If you use Google sign-in, Google tells us your name, email address and a Google account identifier. We use this only to create and sign you in to your InfyExchange account. We do not access your Google contacts, files or any other Google data.

Who can see your information

  • Your name and the items and requests you post are visible to other signed-in users.
  • Your email address and phone number are shown to another user only after an enquiry between you has been accepted, and only to the two people involved.
  • Site administrators can view account details to run the service and handle abuse.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

The app runs on Cloudflare (website), Google Cloud (application server), Neon (database) and Cloudinary (photos). These providers store and process data on our behalf.

Your choices

You can edit your role and joining date in your profile. To have your account and its data deleted, or to ask any question about your data, email vanshgoel15.work@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top.
